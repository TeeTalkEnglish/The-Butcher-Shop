DAY 1 · JOB RESPONSIBILITIES AND HYGIENE

Introduce yourself and talk about the work

Video section (provisional): 00:00–04:26

Open YouTube at 00:00

Shadow a short passage, then practise describing responsibilities and hygiene. The vocabulary terms are in the Vocabulary table.

Day 1 vocabulary: duties · production line · hygiene · quality control

Useful Language

Talking about experience: “My experience includes…” · “My duties were…” · “I have not worked in this field yet, but…”

Talking about motivation: “I am interested in…” · “I would like to learn…” · “I can contribute by…”

Talking about hygiene: “I follow hygiene procedures by…” · “This is important because…”

Interview Questions

1. Can you tell me about yourself?

Sample answer: “I am interested in food processing. I am careful, willing to learn and comfortable working as part of a team.”

2. Why do you want to work in meat processing?

Sample answer: “I am interested in practical work and learning new skills. I would like to contribute to a team and follow the workplace’s safety and quality standards.”

3. What experience or practical skills can you bring to this job?

Sample answer for a new worker: “I have not worked in food processing yet, but I am careful, learn new tasks quickly and am ready to follow the required training.”

Adapt it: If you have relevant experience, describe only your actual duties and skills.

DAY 2 · SAFETY AND PRACTICAL SKILLS

Show that you understand safe work

Video section (provisional): 04:26–08:53

Open YouTube at 04:26

Shadow a short passage and practise speaking about PPE, hazards, injury prevention and practical skills. The vocabulary terms are in the Vocabulary table.

Day 2 vocabulary: hazard · PPE · injury · contamination · cross-contamination · equipment · safe handling

Useful Language

Talking about safety: “Workers must…” · “To reduce the risk of…, workers should…” · “This is important because…”

Talking about PPE: “The required PPE depends on…” · “I would follow the site instructions.”

Talking about skills: “I have experience using…” · “I have not used that equipment yet, so I would ask for training.”

Talking about hygiene: “To prevent cross-contamination, I would…” · “I would follow the workplace procedure.”

Interview Questions

4. What do you know about workplace safety in meat processing?

Sample answer: “Workers should follow their training, use the required PPE and report hazards. This helps reduce risks and protect the team.”

5. What personal protective equipment might you need to wear?

Sample answer: “It depends on the task and workplace. PPE may include protective clothing, gloves or safety footwear. I would follow the site instructions.”

6. How can workers prevent cuts and other injuries?

Sample answer: “Workers should follow their training and the correct procedures. I would not use unfamiliar equipment until I had the required training and permission.”

7. Why is hygiene important, and how can workers prevent cross-contamination?

Sample answer: “Good hygiene helps keep food safe. I would follow the site’s cleaning and handling procedures, use clean equipment as instructed and keep items separate when required.”

DAY 3 · WORKPLACE SITUATIONS AND FINAL PRACTICE

Answer confidently and honestly

Video section (provisional): 08:53–13:19

Open YouTube at 08:53

Review the vocabulary table, shadow a short passage and practise giving an action, a reason and a safe response in each answer.

Day 3 vocabulary: slip hazard · report a hazard · supervisor · authorised · teamwork

Useful Language

Explaining an action: “First, I would…” · “I would keep clear and…” · “I would report it to…”

Giving a reason: “This matters because…” · “This helps reduce the risk of…”

Being honest about experience: “I have not done that task yet, but…” · “I am willing to learn…”

Answer structure: State the action → explain why it matters → describe the procedure you would follow.

Interview Questions

8. What would you do if you noticed an unsafe condition at work?

Sample answer: “I would keep clear of danger, warn people nearby if it was safe, and report it to my supervisor. I would follow the workplace procedure.”

9. Can you follow instructions and work as part of a team?

Sample answer: “Yes. I listen carefully, ask questions when I am unsure and communicate respectfully. I would do my part to support the team.”

10. Why should we hire you?

Sample answer: “I am careful, willing to learn and serious about following instructions. I would work safely and do my best to support the team.”

Adapt it: Add one real strength or example from your own experience.

Final practice: Choose three questions. Answer them before revealing the examples, then try again without reading. Aim for a clear action, a reason and a relevant detail.
