SECTION 3 · IELTS SPEAKING PRACTICE

Original practice questions

These are original IELTS-style prompts inspired by the video's butcher-interview topic, not official IELTS questions. Use your own experience; do not claim skills or work history you do not have.

Part 1 · Short personal questions

Answer each question in a few sentences: answer directly, explain your idea, then give a personal example.

  1. Have you ever prepared for a job interview? What did you do?
  2. What skills do you think are important for a butcher?
  3. Would you prefer to work alone or with a team? Why?
  4. What kind of job would you like to do in the future?

Try this structure: Direct answer → reason or explanation → a brief example from your real life.

Part 2 · Cue card

Describe a job that interests you.

You should say:

  • what the job is
  • what skills or qualities it needs
  • how you learned about it
  • and explain why it interests you

One-minute preparation: Write a few keywords for the job, the skills, how you know about it, and your reason. Do not write full sentences.

Two-minute speaking challenge: Speak for up to two minutes, adding a real example or detail to each point.

Part 3 · Discussion

Give an opinion, explain why, add an example, and finish with a short conclusion.

  1. What qualities make someone effective in a practical job?
  2. How can preparing for an interview help a job applicant?
  3. Should employers focus more on experience or willingness to learn?
  4. How might work skills change as technology develops?

Try this structure: Opinion → explanation → example → conclusion.
